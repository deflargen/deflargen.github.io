Music Uploader

A small personal desktop tool for checking video game music against YouTube's Content ID.

What it does

Music Uploader turns soundtrack files on my own computer into simple test videos and uploads them as private videos to my own YouTube channel. YouTube's Content ID check then shows which songs get copyright claims, so I know which music is safe to use in my videos.

Who it's for

It's a personal tool. It isn't offered to the public, has no accounts or sign-ups, and only ever uploads to the single YouTube channel its owner signs in with.

How it uses Google

It signs in with Google only to upload private videos and to read the name of the signed-in channel, so it can confirm it's uploading to the right one. Details are in the privacy policy.
